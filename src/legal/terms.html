SPI Generator — Terms of Use

SPI Generator is an Excel add-in published by Moran Rocks LLC. It transfers pay item quantities from a Request for Change (RFC) workbook into the Schedule of Payment Items (SPI) workbook open in Excel, changing only the task quantity cells for the pay items listed in the RFC.
