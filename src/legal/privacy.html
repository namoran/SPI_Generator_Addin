SPI Generator — Privacy Policy

SPI Generator is an Excel add-in published by Moran Rocks LLC. It transfers pay item quantities from a Request for Change (RFC) workbook into the Schedule of Payment Items (SPI) workbook open in Excel.

What the add-in does with your files

The RFC file you select is read in the add-in pane on your computer. The add-in writes quantities into the workbook you have open in Excel. Your workbooks and their contents are not uploaded, transmitted, stored, or shared by the add-in.

Data collection

The add-in collects no personal information. It contains no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. It keeps no record of the files you process.

Network activity

The add-in's own files are loaded from its hosting address, and the Microsoft Office JavaScript library is loaded from Microsoft's servers, as with every Office add-in. No workbook data and no personal information is sent in either request.
