RFC Importer — Privacy Policy

RFC Importer is an Excel add-in published by Moran Rocks LLC. It imports pay item quantities from a Request for Change (RFC) workbook into the Schedule of Payment Items (SPI) workbook open in Excel.

What the add-in does with your files

The RFC data you import is read in the add-in pane on your computer, from a workbook file you pick or from the RFC worksheet in the workbook you have open. The add-in writes quantities into that open workbook. Your workbooks and their contents are not uploaded, transmitted, or shared by the add-in.

Data collection

The add-in collects no personal information. It contains no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. When an import succeeds, the add-in saves a small record inside your own workbook (the date, and a fingerprint of the imported tasks, pay items, and quantities). This record never leaves the workbook, and its only purpose is to detect and refuse importing the same RFC data a second time. The add-in itself keeps no files and no separate copies.

Network activity

The add-in's own files are loaded from its hosting address, and the Microsoft Office JavaScript library is loaded from Microsoft's servers, as with every Office add-in. No workbook data and no personal information is sent in either request.
