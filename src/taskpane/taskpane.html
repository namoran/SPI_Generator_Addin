RFC Importer

Imports the line items from a PRP change order (RFC) into the SPI workbook open in Excel. Only the task quantity cells change; Excel recalculates the totals.

  1. Open the SPI workbook in Excel.
  2. Choose where the RFC data comes from, below.
  3. Select Import. The RFC quantities are added into the open workbook.
