SPI Generator

Transfers the line items from a PRP change order (RFC) into the SPI workbook open in Excel. Only the task quantity cells change; Excel recalculates the totals.

  1. Open the SPI workbook in Excel.
  2. Select the RFC workbook below.
  3. Select Generate. The RFC quantities are added into the open workbook.
